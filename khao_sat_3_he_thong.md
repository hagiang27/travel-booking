## Nhan xet chung

Ca 3 he thong deu co chuc nang so sanh gia song song, giup nguoi dung de dang doi chieu nhieu lua chon cung luc. Tuy nhien, diem chung lon nhat va cung la khoang trong ro rang nhat la CA 3 he thong deu KHONG hien thi day du cac khoan phu thu (thue, phi dich vu) ngay tu buoc dau tien - chi hien ra o buoc thanh toan cuoi cung. Rieng Traveloka con co tinh trang gia cuoi cung cao hon han gia ban dau do phat sinh them thue.

Day chinh la van de cot loi ma de tai DT-01 huong toi giai quyet: buoc moi khoan chi phi (gia goc + tat ca phu thu) phai duoc hien thi minh bach ngay tu buoc tim kiem/so sanh dau tien, khong de nguoi dung phai den tan buoc thanh toan moi biet duoc tong chi phi that su.
